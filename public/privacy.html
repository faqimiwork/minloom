Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026

MinLoom is a screen recorder for Google Chrome, made by Nota Tech ("we"). This policy explains what MinLoom does with your information. The short version: MinLoom records and stores everything on your own computer. We do not collect, receive, sell or share your recordings or personal data.

What MinLoom handles, and where it stays

Your screen, tab or windowCaptured only after you start a recording and choose what to share. Processed and saved on your computer.
CameraUsed only to show your camera bubble, or to record you in Camera mode, and only when you choose it. Never sent anywhere.
Microphone and tab soundRecorded into your video only if you turn them on. Never sent anywhere.
Your recordingsStored inside Chrome's storage for the MinLoom extension on your computer, until you delete them or download them. Removing the extension removes them.
SettingsYour choices (for example microphone on or off, bubble shape, quality) are saved in Chrome's extension storage on your computer.
Page titleWhen you record a tab, its title is used as the default name of your video. It stays on your computer.
ScreenshotsTaken only when you click Screenshot. The picture opens in MinLoom's editor on your computer and is never uploaded. Copy it or download it; closing the editor discards it.
Blur boxesThe areas you blur are drawn on your screen only while you record. MinLoom does not read what is underneath them.

What we do not do

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels or advertising.
  • No upload of your videos, audio, camera images or screen to our servers or anyone else's.
  • No reading of the pages you visit, except drawing the camera bubble, countdown and pen on the tab you choose to record.
  • No sale or transfer of any data to third parties.

Chrome permissions, and why MinLoom needs them

tabCapture, desktopCaptureTo record the tab, window or screen you choose.
activeTab, scriptingTo show the camera bubble, countdown, pen and click highlights on the tab you are recording, after you click MinLoom.
offscreenTo run the recorder in the background while you work.
storage, unlimitedStorageTo save your settings and your videos on your computer, including long recordings.
notificationsTo tell you when a recording starts, stops on its own, or needs your attention.
Access to all sites (optional)Asked for only if you turn on "Bubble follows me to other tabs", so the bubble can move with you. You can turn it off any time.

Future features

We plan an optional share link feature. If you choose to use it, the video you choose to share would be uploaded so others can watch it. We will update this policy before that feature is released, and it will never upload anything unless you ask it to.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

MinLoom's use of information complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to provide MinLoom's recording features to you, and is not transferred, sold or used for any other purpose.

Children

MinLoom is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Important changes will also be noted in the extension's release notes.
